# ChatMe-LLM
My journey of building a small LLM from scratch with no prior experience. Learning through YouTube, experimentation, and other LLMs while exploring how language models work, one step at a time.

## ChatMe: Development History

ChatMe is a personal learning project focused on understanding and building a small language model through experimentation.

### Early Development — Mark 1 and Mark 3

The early stages of ChatMe focused on learning how language models work. These versions used text-based datasets while I explored the fundamentals of LLM architecture and training.

### Architecture Experiments — Mark 5 and Mark 6

During this stage, I began experimenting with separate datasets and `.csn` files. Mark 5 used single-head self-attention with Top-k sampling, while Mark 6 introduced multi-head attention with two heads.

### Larger-Scale Experiments — Mark 8 and Mark 9

Starting with Mark 8, I moved towards a larger and more substantial dataset for more serious experiments. Mark 9 continued this development using PyTorch and CUDA, allowing me to train ChatMe on a GPU.

### Ongoing Development

ChatMe is an evolving project. Some earlier versions are no longer available as separate source-code files, but their development history remains part of the project's journey. My goal is to keep learning, experimenting, and improving the model step by step.
